A fast-paced RC car race down a narrow Californian back lane on a sunny day. A blue and yellow miniature buggy speeds away from the camera along a sun-baked paved alley, a weathered stucco building with a leafy tree beside it on the left and a tall red iron spiked security fence running along the right, typical of a Southern California backstreet. The buggy steers hard left the whole time, curving away from the fence and drifting toward the stucco wall on the left side of the lane. Bright midday sun, sharp shadows, dust kicking up behind the tires, the hum of its motor echoing off the walls. If the buggy collides with the wall or tree on the left, it tips over and tumbles onto its side, wheels still spinning.
