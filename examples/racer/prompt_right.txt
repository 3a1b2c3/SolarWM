A fast-paced RC car race down a narrow Californian back lane on a sunny day. A blue and yellow miniature buggy speeds away from the camera along a sun-baked paved alley, then steers hard to the right, its front wheels biting and the rear stepping out in a loose drift as it swings away from the weathered stucco building and leafy tree on the left and arcs toward the tall red iron spiked security fence on the right. Bright midday sun, sharp shadows, dust and grit kicking up from the tires as they scrub sideways, the motor rising in pitch through the turn and echoing off the walls. The camera follows behind, swinging right with the buggy as it holds the line along the sunlit fence side of the alley.
