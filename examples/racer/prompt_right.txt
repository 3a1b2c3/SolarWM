A fast-paced RC car race down a narrow Californian back lane on a sunny day. A blue and yellow miniature buggy speeds away from the camera along a sun-baked paved alley, a weathered stucco building with a leafy tree beside it on the left and a tall red iron spiked security fence running along the right, typical of a Southern California backstreet. The buggy steers hard right the whole time, curving away from the wall and drifting toward the tall red iron spiked security fence on the right side of the lane. Bright midday sun, sharp shadows, dust kicking up behind the tires, the hum of its motor echoing off the walls. If the buggy collides with the fence on the right, it tips over and tumbles onto its side, wheels still spinning.
